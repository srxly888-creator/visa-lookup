Three channels, simply 三通道简明说明

① Visa-free entry / 免签入境No visa, no embassy, no onward ticket. Stay ≤30 days, nationwide.
无需签证、无需使馆、无需联程票；停留 ≤30 天，全国。
② 240h transit / 240小时过境免签Free, no embassy — but needs a confirmed onward ticket to a 3rd country and stays within 24 provinces. ≤10 days.
免费、不用使馆；但需确认联程票，限 24 省，停留 ≤10 天。
③ L tourist visa / L 旅游签证A real visa from a Chinese embassy before travel. Needed if not on any visa-free list.
正式签证，出行前向中国使馆申请。
